### Status Check Functions
* status check functions will controll whether a step or job runs based on what happend before it.
* success()     all previos jobs/steps pass         default, no need to explicityl mention it
* failure()     a previous job/step failed
* always()      no matter what job/stps will run
* cancelled()   workflow is cancelled
